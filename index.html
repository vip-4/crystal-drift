Why it stands out

Small file. Big drift.

01

Blender-crafted 3D

Every model — the teal scout ship, faceted energy crystals, cratered meteors and the amber planet — was built programmatically in Blender 5.2 through the Blender MCP addon and exported as lightweight glTF.

02

Genuinely lightweight

The full game ships as a static HTML/JS bundle with a shared 87 KB of GLB assets — no framework, no runtime, no tracking. It starts in under a second even on slow connections and works offline after first load.

03

Two control schemes

Desktop players use WASD or arrow keys with smooth banking physics. Mobile players get drag-to-fly touch controls. The difficulty scales gently as your score climbs.

Getting started

How to play

  1. Open the game

    Hit the “Play Crystal Drift” button anywhere on this page. The game loads instantly — no wait screen, no installer.

  2. Steer the ship

    On desktop use W / S to climb and dive and A / D to steer; arrow keys work too. On mobile, drag your finger anywhere on the screen.

  3. Collect crystals, dodge meteors

    Fly through the glowing crystal clusters for +10 points each. Touching a meteor costs one of your 3 lives. The field picks up speed the longer you survive.

  4. Beat your best score

    Your high score is saved locally in the browser. Compete against yourself — how long can you keep the drift going?

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Is Crystal Drift free to play?

Yes. Crystal Drift is completely free. It runs in your browser and requires no download, no install and no account.

What are the controls in Crystal Drift?

Use the W and S keys (or up/down arrows) to climb and dive, and A and D keys (or left/right arrows) to steer. On touch devices, drag anywhere on the screen to move the ship.

How does scoring work?

Each energy crystal you collect adds 10 points. Colliding with a meteor costs one life; you start with 3 lives. The game speeds up as your score grows.

What technology does Crystal Drift use?

Crystal Drift is a static site built with Three.js (WebGL) and glTF/GLB 3D assets created in Blender. The whole game downloads in under 100 KB, so it starts almost instantly.

Can I play Crystal Drift on mobile?

Yes. Crystal Drift works on phones and tablets via touch drag controls, and on desktop via keyboard.
